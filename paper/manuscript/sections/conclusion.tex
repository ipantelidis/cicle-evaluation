\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

How a small classifier narrows the label space matters once the labelled pool is long-tailed. Class-conditional conformal sets keep the rare classes, top-$m$, probability-mass and marginal sets lose them, and the LLM cannot recover a label it was not offered. Rule: calibrate per class whenever the pool is skewed.

Narrowing gives a small, reliable gain over few-shot prompting and a shorter prompt, most when label names carry no meaning. Rule: expect a point or two and a third fewer tokens, and more when labels are codes. % PENDING: MASSIVE

An LLM pipeline pays when labels are skewed or scarce, and a fine-tuned encoder or the base classifier wins when they are plentiful and balanced. Rule: fine-tune on a large balanced pool, otherwise prompt with a narrowed list and loosen $\alpha$ as the classifier improves.

Prompts with different label information compare formats, not methods. Rule: list the admissible labels, or the candidate set for very large label spaces, in every prompt.
