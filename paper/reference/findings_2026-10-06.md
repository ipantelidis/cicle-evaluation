# Findings so far (fixed pipeline, 6 small LLMs x 3 seeds unless noted) — written 2026-10-06 midday

Pipeline fixes vs the DS2026 paper: prompts now list the allowed labels; same wording for few-shot and CICLe;
same 1,600-example retrieval pool for both; seeded conformal sets; normalised output parsing; per-instance
records; macro-F1 over classes present in the test sample. Invalid outputs fell from 20-35% to 0-6%.
The old "model heterogeneity" (Ministral/Qwen hurt by CICLe) was an invalid-output artefact.

Reference setting: MiniLM embeddings, logistic regression, alpha=0.05, k in {1,2,4,8} (main grid) or {1,4} (everything else).
Datasets: Yahoo (10 cls, balanced), SST-5 (5), SemEval-18 emoji (20, 8.7x), GoEmotions (28, 185x), Ohsumed (23, ~65x, ~190-word abstracts).
Variants: Fixed (k most similar examples from allowed classes) and Per-Class (k per allowed class).

1. CICLe vs few-shot (paired bootstrap over test instances, 72 pairs): significant gains in 7/8 cells, +0.5 to +2.0 pp.
   Yahoo Fixed -0.1 (n.s.), Yahoo PC +1.4; SST Fixed +2.0, PC +1.3; SemEval +0.5/+0.5; GoEmotions +0.6/+0.9; Ohsumed Fixed +1.15.
   CICLe PC uses 13-36% fewer prompt tokens than few-shot PC. Fixed beats PC on GoEmotions (26.6 vs 25.9 at a sixth of the tokens).
2. Imbalance does NOT increase CICLe's edge over few-shot (controlled Yahoo: +1.4 at 1x, +1.1 at 10x, +0.9 at 100x for PC).
   The old paper's "imbalance predicts CICLe's benefit" claim is dead.
3. How you narrow matters under imbalance. CICLe minus top-k (same mean set size), PC: Yahoo 1x +0.8, 10x +1.7, 100x +7.4;
   SST 1x +0.3 (n.s.), 10x +2.4, 100x +4.2; GoEmotions +2.5; Ohsumed +1.1; SemEval 0.0. Minus probability-mass: up to +9.4 (Yahoo 100x), +10.7 (SST 100x).
   Coverage at 100x on Yahoo: conformal 96.7%, top-k 81.6%, mass 74.2%. Marginal (non class-conditional) CP: 62.9% at set size 4.1 (sets only, LLM runs pending).
4. Supervised baselines. MiniLM+LR alone: Yahoo 65.4 (beats every LLM pipeline), SST 35.5, SemEval 9.7, GoEmotions 11.2, Ohsumed 45.2.
   Fine-tuned RoBERTa-base (3 seeds): Yahoo 61.8, SST 51.2, SemEval 24.5, GoEmotions 37.0, Ohsumed 55.4; best LLM-pipeline 6-model mean: 63.8, 47.6, 16.1, 27.3, 51.7.
   Under 100x imbalance: RoBERTa 41.2 (Yahoo) / 31.9 (SST) vs best LLM pipeline 56.0 / 43.4; MiniLM+LR 31.4 / 12.6.
   => fine-tuning wins with balanced data; in-context pipelines degrade far less when the labelled pool is skewed.
5. Label renaming (nonsense words): CICLe minus few-shot on Yahoo +6.5 (Fixed) / +5.6 (PC); GoEmotions +0.7/+1.0. Few-shot Fixed k=1 on Yahoo: 25.3 vs CICLe 34.3.
6. Ablations (Llama-3.1-8B + Llama-3.2-3B): alpha looser helps except SST (alpha=0.2: Yahoo +3.6 over few-shot, GoEmotions +3.0, SemEval +1.4; LLM skipped 35% on Yahoo at 0.2, ~0% elsewhere);
   Contriever > MiniLM on SST/GoEmotions, < on Yahoo; TF-IDF weakest; SVM = LR.
7. Larger models (seed 42, partial): Qwen-32B zero-shot Yahoo 60.7 / SST 48.8 vs best 3B+CICLe PC 61.8 / 48.1; CICLe adds 1-3 pp to the 32B and 12B models too.
8. Conformal sets: coverage 94-96% at alpha=0.05 but large on many-class data (22/28 GoEmotions, 17/20 SemEval); LLM skipped <=3.4%.
9. Model heterogeneity now: PC never significantly hurts any model; Fixed hurts Llama-3.2-3B (-2.3) and Mistral-7B (-0.7) on Yahoo only.
